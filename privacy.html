Privacy Policy for Dude Jump

Effective date: May 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dude Jump ("the Game", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you play the Game, including when the Game is available through CrazyGames.

Developer note: before public release, replace this note with your real contact email or support URL.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not intentionally collect your name, email address, or phone number.

When you play the Game, the following technical and gameplay information may be collected automatically:

  • Device and browser information, such as browser type, operating system, language, screen size, and user agent.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Gameplay events, such as game loading, entering the lobby, entering a level, game over, score, ad-related gameplay flow, and revive usage.
  • Crash or error information, such as error messages and stack traces, to help us diagnose issues.
  • Cookies or similar identifiers used by analytics and platform providers.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Understand how players interact with the Game.
  • Improve gameplay, performance, stability, and loading experience.
  • Detect and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • Support ads and platform features where applicable.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game may use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics for Firebase for analytics and event reporting. Google may process analytics data according to Google's privacy terms. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.
  • CrazyGames for game hosting, platform features, advertising, and related services. Learn more: CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Firebase, Google, CrazyGames, and other platform services may use cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, or similar technologies to provide analytics, remember technical identifiers, deliver platform services, and support advertising.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by law, analytics and advertising-related processing may rely on your consent. CrazyGames and other distribution platforms may provide consent management tools. Essential technical processing may be necessary to provide and secure the Game.

6. Data Sharing

We may share technical and gameplay data with the third-party services listed above to operate, analyze, improve, and monetize the Game. We do not sell your personal information directly.

7. Children's Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict the processing of your personal information. You may also be able to control cookies and tracking technologies through your browser, device settings, or the platform's consent tools.

9. Data Retention

Analytics and technical data are retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or according to the retention settings of our third-party service providers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
